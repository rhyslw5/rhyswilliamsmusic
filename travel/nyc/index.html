NYC - City of Smoke and Mirrors (unfinished)

May 26, 2025 - New York City, United States

CHAPTER 1: HARD ROCK HOTEL

 This trip originated from my mom being asked to review the Hard Rock Hotel in New York City for Good Housekeeping magazine. This was far from my first time being to New York City, but there's so much to do, it never gets old.
 After unpacking our luggage, we quickly realized how mismanaged this hotel was. All the hotel-specific TV channels had different hours and instructions for the same activities. We were supposed to review specific activities the hotel offered, but doing anything at this hotel proved impossible, as none of the staff seemed to know what was going on.
 After sufficiently checking out our room, we headed downstairs for a drink. I ordered a drink called "Lovers and Friends" that supposedly had pandan liqueur in it, but while the bartender was making it, I heard him mutter under his breath, "I wish we had pandan liqueur". He still served it to me without saying anything, and when I asked him if there was any pandan liqueur in it, he told me there was a shortage and he had substituted it with banana liqueur. Besides the substitution making no sense flavor-wise, I could've had a banana allergy or something, so that gave me very bad vibes about the hotel.
 After the drinks, the manager took us on a tour of the hotel. We got to see a pretty decked-out music studio (that costs extra) and a cool rooftop bar (that was closed the entire time we were there). For dinner, we had a charcuterie plate (without cheese), ridiculously huge espresso martinis, and I got mac & cheese, which they somehow messed up by putting way too much truffle in. At least the chocolate lava cake for dessert was alright.

CHAPTER 2: THE GREAT GATSBY

 We saw "The Great Gatsby" on Broadway. It was pretty good. There were even free drink refills at intermission!
